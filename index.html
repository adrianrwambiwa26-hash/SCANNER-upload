Follow Up Sheet

Scan a connection card and send the reviewed details to Google Sheets.

1. Choose the service

The service choice will be saved in a new “Service” column. Please add that header in column M of your sheet.

2. Upload a photo of the card

Handwriting recognition can make mistakes. Always check the fields below before submitting.

3. Review the extracted details

Privacy tip: use this only with your church's approval. Card photos may contain personal information. The scanner sends the image to the configured AI vision service for reading; avoid keeping photos longer than needed.

Setup required: deploy the included Google Apps Script as a web app, then paste its URL into the configuration below in this HTML file.
